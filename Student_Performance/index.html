💡 Why Gradient Boosting Outperforms Linear Models

1. Non-linear Interaction Thresholds: In student performance dynamics, the penalty for excessive absences or past failures is non-linear. While a linear model applies a flat slope, decision trees split at critical tipping points (e.g., absences > 12 days).

2. Synergistic Momentum: The engineered feature Academic Momentum (G2 − G1) combines non-linearly with G2. Students exhibiting upward trajectory despite a low G1 are effectively separated by boosted splits without suffering linear drag.

3. Residual Minimization: Gradient boosting builds sequential estimators that directly target the errors made by previous iterations, achieving a sub-1.0 point MAE (0.9942) on held-out validation folds.

ℹ️ About ZENITH

Comprehensive information on the mission, algorithms, methodology, and technology stack powering ZENITH.

🎯 Mission & Scope

ZENITH was developed to provide educators, counselors, and academic institutions with early warning indicators and personalized diagnostic signals for secondary school student outcomes.

By analyzing assessment records alongside socio-demographic indicators and attendance rates, ZENITH detects negative trends before final exams occur, allowing tailored interventions (such as targeted tutoring and attendance check-ins) to be enacted when they are most effective.

🔬 Machine Learning Methodology

  • Feature Engineering: We designed two domain-specific composite features: Academic_Momentum (Δ between period 1 and 2 assessments) and Effort_Absence_Ratio.
  • Preprocessing Pipeline: Missing values are imputed with median/mode, numeric indicators normalized via StandardScaler, and categorical levels encoded through sparse-tolerant one-hot transformers.
  • Iterative Residual Minimization: The Gradient Boosting algorithm minimizes squared error loss using stagewise additive regression stumps.
  • Evaluation & Integrity: Zero leakage is enforced; target G3 is strictly isolated from predictors, and 20% validation splits ensure out-of-sample generalizability.
