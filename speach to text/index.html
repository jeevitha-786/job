STEP 1 EXPLORE CAPABILITIES

What is in VocaElite Speech Studio?

Explore the audio and speech capabilities packed into this intelligent studio:

🔊
Text to Speech (TTS)

Convert typed or pasted text into lifelike natural voice with customizable speech speed, pitch, and volume.

🎙
Speech to Text Dictation (STT)

Speak directly into your microphone for real-time live transcription with instant copy and .txt file download.

🌐
Bilingual Engine (English & Tamil)

Full native support to use the app in English or தமிழ் with speech recognition and synthesis for both languages.

⏱
Persistent Work History

All your generated speeches and dictated transcripts are automatically saved in the left sidebar with 1-click restore.

🤟
Sign Language to Voice & Text

AI camera gesture recognition translates real-time hand signs into written sentences and natural vocal speech.
